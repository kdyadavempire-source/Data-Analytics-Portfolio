# Sales & Revenue Analytics Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

This project analyzes sales transaction data to evaluate revenue performance, customer behavior, product performance, regional trends, and overall business growth.

The project combines SQL, Python, Excel, and Power BI to perform data cleaning, exploratory analysis, KPI calculation, visualization, and business reporting.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

* Analyze total sales and revenue performance
* Identify top-performing products and categories
* Analyze customer purchasing behavior
* Evaluate regional sales performance
* Identify monthly and yearly revenue trends
* Track key business performance indicators
* Generate actionable business insights
